Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers to help you plan your next step with confidence.

Do I need to know Korean to study in Korea?
Requirements depend on the selected program and track. English Track programs may not require a Korean exam, while Korean Track programs have Korean-language requirements for progression.
What is the difference between D-2 and D-4?
D-2 is generally for degree-level study. D-4 is generally used for language training.
Can I work while studying?
Eligible international students may be permitted to work part-time subject to Korean immigration rules, permission requirements, university policies, and applicable restrictions. Employment is not guaranteed.
How much money do I need to show?
Financial requirements can vary depending on university, visa type, location, and current immigration requirements.
When can I apply?
Degree and language programs have different semester schedules and application periods. Speak with our team to confirm the intake that fits your timeline.
Do you help with visa processing?
Study and Work provides application and visa guidance but does not guarantee visa approval.
What happens if my visa is denied?
Refund and tuition policies depend on the applicable agreement, university policy, and payment terms.
